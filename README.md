# Oncordo

**A unified operations management platform for physical service-based businesses.**

Oncordo helps businesses manage their daily operations through a single platform — connecting bookings, walk-in customers, service delivery, staff, resources, and payments.

Instead of managing different parts of the business with disconnected tools or manual processes, Oncordo brings essential operations together in one place.

## 🎯 Vision

Make everyday business operations simpler, more organized, and easier to manage.

Oncordo aims to help business owners focus less on administrative work and more on delivering great customer experiences.

## 🏢 Target Businesses

Oncordo is being designed for physical service-based businesses, initially focusing on:

- **Indoor Sports Facilities** — Badminton, table tennis, futsal, squash, and other sports venues.
- **Training Centers** — Programming institutes, coaching centers, language schools, and professional training institutes.

The platform is designed with flexibility in mind so that additional business types can be supported in the future.

## ✨ Core Capabilities

- **Customer Management** — Maintain customer profiles, contact information, and activity history.
- **Booking Management** — Handle online reservations, scheduling, and booking status.
- **Walk-in Management** — Register and manage customers who arrive without prior reservations.
- **Visit Tracking** — Track actual customer visits and their activities.
- **Service Sessions** — Manage scheduled and ongoing services, including staff and resource assignments.
- **Staff Management** — Organize staff profiles, responsibilities, and availability.
- **Resource Management** — Manage courts, rooms, equipment, and other bookable resources.
- **Course Management** — Organize courses, batches, enrollments, class sessions, and attendance.
- **Order & Payment Management** — Track orders, payments, and financial transactions.

## 🏗️ Architecture

Oncordo follows an operations-centric approach.

Its core concepts include:

- **Booking:** A planned reservation.
- **Visit:** An actual customer arrival at a business location.
- **Service Session:** The actual delivery of a service.
- **Order:** A record of products or services purchased.
- **Payment:** A record of money received against an order.
- **Course Enrollment:** A customer's enrollment in a course or batch.

These concepts are managed independently but work together to represent real business workflows.
